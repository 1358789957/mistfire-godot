Clamp / towers
--------------
Player + tower placement use point-in-polygon (ray cast) plus distance-to-coast.
Island.contains_xz / Island.contains_v / Island.clamp_xz.
If outside or within 1.25 m of the water, snap to the nearest coast point then
step inward along the edge normal (fallback: binary search toward the altar).
NOT a circle radius.

Mesh
----
Land is a MultiMesh of ~4 m grass/sand boxes clipped to the polygon
(431 grass + 82 sand) plus 114 dirt cliff boxes along the coast. Runtime
ArrayMesh was invisible on llvmpipe+gl_compatibility, so tiles match the
road path that already renders. Cheap height: woods tiles sit a bit higher,
beach at 0.02, village 0. Foam strip follows the true polygon.
Walk collider stays a flat box; roads stay walkable. Player clamp is PIP.

Minimap
-------
Fills the triangulated polygon (not a green disc), sand polyline, roads, icons.
